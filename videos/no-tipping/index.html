Don't leave a tip — here's why

Tipping isn't customary in Japan. Show thanks with words instead.

Key points

  • Pay the bill amount
  • Say ごちそうさまでした
  • Use the cash tray

Transcript

  1. お会計お願いします。

    Okaikei onegaishimasu.

    The bill, please.

  2. チップは置かなくて大丈夫。

    Chippu wa okanakute daijoubu.

    You don't need to leave a tip.

  3. ごちそうさまでした！

    Gochisousama deshita!

    Thank you for the meal!
