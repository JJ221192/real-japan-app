"Sore na" — the one-word agreement

それな is a quick, casual way to agree. Great with friends, odd with your boss.

Key points

  • Strong casual agreement
  • Use そうですね in polite settings

Transcript

  1. 「この店、また来たいね」

    "Kono mise, mata kitai ne"

    "I want to come back to this place."

  2. 「それな！」

    "Sore na!"

    "Totally!"

  3. 丁寧に言うなら「そうですね」。

    Teinei ni iu nara "sou desu ne".

    Politely: "sou desu ne".
