Why is everyone so quiet on the train?

Commuter trains are shared quiet spaces. Small habits make a big difference.

Key points

  • No phone calls
  • Backpack in front when crowded
  • Priority seats

Transcript

  1. 電車の中では電話しない。

    Densha no naka de wa denwa shinai.

    No phone calls on the train.

  2. リュックは前に。

    Ryukku wa mae ni.

    Wear your backpack in front.

  3. 優先席はゆずろう。

    Yuusenseki wa yuzurou.

    Give up priority seats to those who need them.
