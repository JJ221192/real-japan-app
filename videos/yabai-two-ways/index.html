"Yabai" can mean amazing OR terrible

やばい started as 'dangerous' but young people use it for anything extreme — good or bad. Listen to the tone and the situation.

Key points

  • Positive: food, views, surprises
  • Negative: being late, mistakes
  • Too casual for staff

Transcript

  1. このラーメン、やばい！

    Kono ramen, yabai!

    This ramen is amazing!

  2. やばい、財布忘れた。

    Yabai, saifu wasureta.

    Oh no, I forgot my wallet.

  3. 同じ言葉、反対の意味。

    Onaji kotoba, hantai no imi.

    Same word, opposite meanings.

  4. 声のトーンで分かるよ。

    Koe no toon de wakaru yo.

    You can tell from the tone.
