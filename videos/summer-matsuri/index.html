A summer night at the shrine festival

Shrine festivals turn the grounds into a night market. Here's what you'll see and hear.

Key points

  • 屋台 = food stalls
  • Bring cash
  • Follow staff around the mikoshi

Transcript

  1. お祭り、めっちゃ人多い！

    Omatsuri, meccha hito ooi!

    The festival is so crowded!

  2. 屋台で何食べる？

    Yatai de nani taberu?

    What should we eat at the stalls?

  3. 提灯がきれいだね。

    Chouchin ga kirei da ne.

    The lanterns are beautiful.
