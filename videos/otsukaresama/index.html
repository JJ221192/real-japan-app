Why do Japanese people say "otsukaresama"?

お疲れ様です is used to show appreciation for someone's effort. You'll hear it at work, school, and even among friends.

Key points

  • Used when someone has finished work or done their best
  • Shows respect and care

Transcript

  1. お疲れ様です！

    Otsukaresama desu!

    Good job! / Thanks for your hard work

  2. 仕事の終わりによく聞く言葉。

    Shigoto no owari ni yoku kiku kotoba.

    You hear it a lot after work.

  3. 友達同士なら「お疲れ」。

    Tomodachi doushi nara "otsukare".

    Between friends: "otsukare".

  4. 相手の努力へのリスペクト。

    Aite no doryoku e no risupekuto.

    It's respect for someone's effort.
