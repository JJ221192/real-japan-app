I got 大吉! — drawing an omikuji

Omikuji are paper fortunes found at shrines and temples. Here's how to read the rank and what people do with bad ones.

Key points

  • 大吉 = great blessing
  • 凶 = bad luck
  • Tie bad fortunes at the rack

Transcript

  1. おみくじ、引いてみよう。

    Omikuji, hiite miyou.

    Let's draw a fortune.

  2. やった、大吉！

    Yatta, daikichi!

    Yes, great blessing!

  3. 凶だったら、ここに結ぼう。

    Kyou dattara, koko ni musubou.

    If it's bad luck, tie it here.
