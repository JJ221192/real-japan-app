Why are there so many torii?

At some shrines, worshippers and businesses donate torii gates as thanks or with a wish, creating long tunnels of vermilion.

Key points

  • Torii mark the entrance to sacred space
  • Some are donated
  • Walk at the side of the path

Transcript

  1. 鳥居がずっと続いてる！

    Torii ga zutto tsuzuiteru!

    The gates go on and on!

  2. 奉納された鳥居なんだって。

    Hounou sareta torii nan datte.

    They say these gates were donated.

  3. 道の真ん中は避けて歩こう。

    Michi no mannaka wa sakete arukou.

    Let's walk to the side of the path.
