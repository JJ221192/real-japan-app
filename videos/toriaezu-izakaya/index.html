"Toriaezu" at the izakaya

とりあえず means 'for now'. At an izakaya, groups often start with a quick first drink before looking at the menu.

Key points

  • 'For now' / 'to start with'
  • Expect a small otoshi dish with your first drink

Transcript

  1. すみません、注文お願いします。

    Sumimasen, chuumon onegaishimasu.

    Excuse me, we'd like to order.

  2. とりあえず、ビール二つで。

    Toriaezu, biiru futatsu de.

    Two beers to start, please.

  3. これは「お通し」です。

    Kore wa "otooshi" desu.

    This is the 'otoshi' appetizer.

  4. では、いただきます！

    Dewa, itadakimasu!

    Well then, let's eat!
