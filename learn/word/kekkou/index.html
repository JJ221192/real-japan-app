Be careful

Can sound cold when declining something kind. 大丈夫です is softer.

Meanings & examples

  1. 1No, thank you (firm)

    Declining clearly, for example a street salesperson.

    • 勧誘は結構です。

      Kanyuu wa kekkou desu.

      No thank you, I'm not interested.

    Polite alternative: 大丈夫です（daijoubu desu）

  2. 2That's fine / good

    Accepting something as acceptable.

    • カードで結構です。

      Kaado de kekkou desu.

      Card is fine.
