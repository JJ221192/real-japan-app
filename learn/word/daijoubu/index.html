大丈夫です

daijoubu desuだいじょうぶです

Polite Safe to usevery commonnationwide

I'm OK / No, thank you

Audio isn't available: no Japanese voice was found on this device.

Be careful

Because it can mean yes or no, add a small hand wave or 'いりません' if you mean no.

Meanings & examples

  1. 1I'm fine / It's OK

    Reassuring someone.

    • ちょっと転んだけど、大丈夫です。

      Chotto koronda kedo, daijoubu desu.

      I tripped a little, but I'm OK.

  2. 2No, thank you

    Declining an offer, for example a bag at a shop.

    • 「袋はご利用ですか？」「大丈夫です」

      "Fukuro wa goriyou desu ka?" "Daijoubu desu"

      "Would you like a bag?" "No, thank you."
