Excuse me / Sorry / Thank you

Audio isn't available: no Japanese voice was found on this device.

Meanings & examples

  1. 1Excuse me (getting attention)

    Calling staff at a restaurant or stopping someone to ask a question.

    • すみません、注文お願いします。

      Sumimasen, chuumon onegaishimasu.

      Excuse me, we'd like to order.

  2. 2Sorry

    A light apology.

    • 遅れてすみません。

      Okurete sumimasen.

      Sorry I'm late.

  3. 3Thank you (for the trouble)

    When someone does something for you.

    • わざわざすみません。

      Wazawaza sumimasen.

      Thank you for going out of your way.
