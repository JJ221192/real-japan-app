Meanings & examples

  1. 1Let's eat / I humbly receive

    Said before starting a meal, often with hands together.

    • おいしそう！いただきます。

      Oishisou! Itadakimasu.

      Looks delicious! Let's eat.

  2. 2I'll gladly take it

    Accepting food or a drink someone offers.

    • では、お茶をいただきます。

      Dewa, ocha o itadakimasu.

      Then I'll have some tea, thank you.
