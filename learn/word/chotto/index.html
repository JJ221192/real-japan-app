Be careful

If someone answers with ちょっと… and stops, they are probably saying no. Don't push.

Meanings & examples

  1. 1A little

    Literal meaning.

    • ちょっと待ってください。

      Chotto matte kudasai.

      Please wait a moment.

  2. 2That's a bit difficult (polite refusal)

    Trailing off after ちょっと usually means 'no'.

    • 「明日はどう？」「明日はちょっと…」

      "Ashita wa dou?" "Ashita wa chotto..."

      "How about tomorrow?" "Tomorrow is a bit... (no)"
