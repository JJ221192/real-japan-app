Meanings & examples

  1. 1Thank you for the meal

    Said after eating, also to restaurant staff when leaving.

    • ごちそうさまでした。おいしかったです。

      Gochisousama deshita. Oishikatta desu.

      Thank you for the meal. It was delicious.

    • 先輩、ごちそうさまでした！

      Senpai, gochisousama deshita!

      Thanks for treating me!
