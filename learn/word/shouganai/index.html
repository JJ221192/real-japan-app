Meanings & examples

  1. 1It can't be helped

    Accepting a situation you can't change.

    • 雨ならしょうがないね。

      Ame nara shouganai ne.

      If it's raining, it can't be helped.

    • 売り切れか、しょうがない。

      Urikire ka, shouganai.

      Sold out, oh well.
