Visiting a shrine or temple to pray

Audio isn't available: no Japanese voice was found on this device.

Meanings & examples

  1. 1A visit to pray at a shrine or temple

    Used for both shrines and temples, and also for visiting a family grave.

    • 朝、近くの神社にお参りした。

      Asa, chikaku no jinja ni omairi shita.

      I went to pray at a nearby shrine this morning.

    • お参りの前に手を清めます。

      Omairi no mae ni te o kiyomemasu.

      You purify your hands before you pray.
