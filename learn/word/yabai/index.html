やばい

yabai

Casual Be carefulvery commonnationwide

Amazing / Terrible / This is too much (depends on the situation)

Who says it: Very common among teens to people in their 40s

Audio isn't available: no Japanese voice was found on this device.

Be careful

Avoid: In formal situations, with hotel staff, or when talking to older strangers.

Good for: With friends and people your own age.

Meanings & examples

  1. 1Amazing, incredible

    Reacting to something surprisingly good, like food or a view.

    • このラーメン、やばい！

      Kono ramen, yabai!

      This ramen is amazing!

    • 夜景やばくない？

      Yakei yabakunai?

      Isn't this night view incredible?

  2. 2Bad, dangerous, in trouble

    When something has gone wrong or is about to.

    • やばい、終電に乗り遅れた。

      Yabai, shuuden ni noriokureta.

      Oh no, I missed the last train.

    Polite alternative: 困りました（komarimashita）
