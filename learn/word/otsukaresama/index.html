お疲れ様です

otsukaresama desuおつかれさまです

Polite Safe to usevery commonnationwide

Good job / Thanks for your hard work

Audio isn't available: no Japanese voice was found on this device.

Good for: At work, in clubs, after a shared activity. Friends shorten it to お疲れ (otsukare).

Meanings & examples

  1. 1Thanks for your hard work

    Said to someone who has finished work or done their best.

    • 今日も一日お疲れ様です。

      Kyou mo ichinichi otsukaresama desu.

      Thanks for all your hard work today.

  2. 2Hello (greeting between coworkers)

    Used as a greeting in the office or on work chats, even in the morning.

    • お疲れ様です。資料を送りました。

      Otsukaresama desu. Shiryou o okurimashita.

      Hi there. I've sent the documents.
