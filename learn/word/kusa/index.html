LOL (internet slang)

Who says it: Mostly online / younger users

Audio isn't available: no Japanese voice was found on this device.

Be careful

Avoid: Out loud or in any polite message. It is written slang.

Meanings & examples

  1. 1LOL

    Online only. Comes from 'w' (warai = laugh) which looks like grass when repeated.

    • 猫が電車に乗ってて草

      Neko ga densha ni nottete kusa

      A cat riding the train, LOL

    • 自販機でおでん売ってて草

      Jihanki de oden uttete kusa

      They sell oden in a vending machine, LOL
