Meanings & examples

  1. 1Seriously? Really?

    Reacting with surprise.

    • マジで？もう売り切れ？

      Maji de? Mou urikire?

      Seriously? Already sold out?

    Polite alternative: 本当ですか（hontou desu ka）

  2. 2Really, very (intensifier)

    Adding emphasis to an adjective.

    • マジで寒い。

      Maji de samui.

      It's really cold.
