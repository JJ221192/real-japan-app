ご利益

goriyakuごりやく

Neutral Safe to usecommonnationwide

The blessing or benefit a shrine is known for

Audio isn't available: no Japanese voice was found on this device.

Meanings & examples

  1. 1Divine blessing, benefit

    Shrines are often known for a particular blessing, like study, love or safe travel.

    • この神社は学業のご利益があるらしい。

      Kono jinja wa gakugyou no goriyaku ga aru rashii.

      This shrine is said to bring luck with studies.

    • ご利益がありますように。

      Goriyaku ga arimasu you ni.

      May it bring you good fortune.
