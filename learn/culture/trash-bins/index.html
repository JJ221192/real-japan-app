Where are all the trash bins?

ゴミ箱が少ない理由

Last updated: 2026-10-12Cultural review: pending (sample content)

Overview

  • Public trash bins are fewer than many visitors expect.
  • Many people take their trash home and sort it there.
  • Bins you do find are often split by type: burnable, cans, bottles, PET.

What travelers should know

  • Carry a small bag for your own trash.
  • Convenience stores and vending machines often have bins for items bought there.
  • Read the pictures on bins and separate bottles, caps and labels where asked.

Exceptions & regional differences

Sorting rules differ by city, so a hotel's rules may not match the last place you stayed.
