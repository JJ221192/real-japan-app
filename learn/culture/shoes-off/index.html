When do you take your shoes off?

靴を脱ぐ場所

5 min readLast updated: 2026-10-12Cultural review: pending (sample content)

Overview

  • Many indoor spaces separate 'outside' from 'inside' with a step called genkan.
  • If the floor rises or you see shoe shelves or slippers, take your shoes off.
  • Never step on tatami mats with slippers.

What travelers should know

  • Wear socks without holes when visiting temples or traditional restaurants.
  • Use the separate toilet slippers only inside the toilet room.
  • In fitting rooms, step out of your shoes before going on the raised floor.

How to do it

  1. 1Turn your shoes to face the door after taking them off, if there is space.

Exceptions & regional differences

Many modern restaurants and offices keep shoes on. Follow what others are doing.
