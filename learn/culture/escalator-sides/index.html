Which side of the escalator?

エスカレーターの立ち位置

3 min readLast updated: 2026-10-12Cultural review: pending (sample content)

Overview

  • Traditionally people in the Tokyo area stand on the left, and in the Osaka area on the right.
  • Many stations now ask people to stand still on both sides for safety.
  • Walking on escalators is discouraged in several prefectures.

What travelers should know

  • Follow posted signs and what people around you are doing.

Exceptions & regional differences

Customs vary by city and are changing. Signs at the station take priority.

Japanese you'll hear

  • すみませんsumimasen · Excuse me / Sorry / Thank you
