Should you tip in Japan?

チップの習慣

4 min readLast updated: 2026-10-12Cultural review: pending (sample content)

Overview

  • Tipping is not part of everyday restaurant or taxi culture.
  • Good service is considered part of the job, and staff may chase you to return the money.
  • Some upscale places add a service charge to the bill instead.

What travelers should know

  • Pay the amount on the bill, usually at the register near the exit.
  • Say ごちそうさまでした when you leave to show thanks.
  • In many places you put cash on the small tray instead of handing it over directly.

How to do it

  1. 1Ask for the bill with お会計お願いします (okaikei onegaishimasu).

Exceptions & regional differences

Traditional inns and private guides are occasionally given a gift of money in an envelope, but it's never required.
