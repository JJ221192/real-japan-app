Why are trains so quiet?

電車の中のマナー

Last updated: 2026-10-12Cultural review: pending (sample content)

Overview

  • Talking on the phone inside trains is widely considered rude.
  • People talk quietly, and many set phones to silent mode.
  • Priority seats are for elderly, pregnant, injured or disabled passengers.

What travelers should know

  • Hold your backpack in front of you or put it on the rack when it's crowded.
  • Keep conversations low.
  • Eating on commuter trains is usually avoided; long-distance trains are different.

How to do it

  1. If you must take a call, say you'll call back and get off at the next stop.

Exceptions & regional differences

Shinkansen and long-distance trains allow eating; local commuter lines generally don't.
