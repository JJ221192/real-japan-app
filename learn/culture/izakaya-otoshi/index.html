Why did I get a dish I didn't order?

居酒屋のお通し

Last updated: 2026-10-12Cultural review: pending (sample content)

Overview

  • Many izakaya bring a small dish called お通し (otoshi) with your first drink.
  • It usually works like a table charge and appears on the bill.
  • It's normal, not a mistake or a scam.

What travelers should know

  • Some places let you decline it, but many don't — it's part of the seating charge.
  • Ordering the first round together with とりあえず is common.
  • Ask staff with すみません when you want to order.

How to do it

  1. Check the menu for a note like お通し代 (otoshi-dai) if you want to know the price.

Exceptions & regional differences

Not every izakaya charges it, and the price varies by place.
