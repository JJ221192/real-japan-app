How to visit a Shinto shrine

神社のお参り

6 min readLast updated: 2026-10-12Cultural review: pending (sample content)

Overview

  • Shrines are active places of worship, not only sightseeing spots.
  • Many visitors rinse their hands, offer a coin and pray with bows and claps.
  • Nobody expects visitors to be perfect; a respectful attitude matters most.

What travelers should know

  • Bow lightly at the torii gate before entering.
  • Walk at the side of the main path; the center is traditionally for the deities.
  • Check signs before taking photos of the main hall.

How to do it

  1. 1At the water basin, rinse your left hand, then your right, then your mouth using your hand (not the ladle).
  2. 2At the hall: offer a coin, bow twice, clap twice, pray, bow once.

Exceptions & regional differences

Some shrines use different bowing and clapping patterns. Temples (Buddhist) are different: you usually don't clap.

Japanese you'll hear

  • すみませんsumimasen · Excuse me / Sorry / Thank you
