The convenience store survival guide

コンビニの使い方

Last updated: 2026-10-12Cultural review: pending (sample content)

Overview

  • Convenience stores sell meals, drinks, tickets and daily essentials 24 hours a day in many areas.
  • Staff may ask if you need a bag, chopsticks or if you want your food heated.
  • Plastic bags usually cost a few yen.

What travelers should know

  • Learn to recognize 袋いりますか？ (Do you need a bag?) and 温めますか？ (Shall I heat it?).
  • Put your cash or card on the tray at the register.
  • Eating inside is possible only where there is an eat-in corner.

How to do it

  1. Reply はい (yes) or 大丈夫です (no thanks).

Exceptions & regional differences

Services, payment options and eat-in areas differ between chains and locations.
