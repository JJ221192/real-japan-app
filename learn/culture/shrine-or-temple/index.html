Shrine or temple — what's the difference?

神社とお寺の違い

5 min readLast updated: 2026-10-12Cultural review: pending (sample content)

Overview

  • Shrines (神社, jinja) belong to Shinto; temples (寺, tera) belong to Buddhism.
  • A torii gate usually means a shrine. Temples tend to have large wooden gates, pagodas and incense burners.
  • Many people in Japan visit both, depending on the occasion.

What travelers should know

  • At shrines, people often bow twice, clap twice and bow once.
  • At temples, people usually put their hands together quietly — no clapping.
  • Names help too: places ending in 神社 or 大社 are shrines; 寺 or 院 are temples.

Exceptions & regional differences

The line is not always sharp: some temples have small shrines inside, and some shrines have Buddhist elements from history.
