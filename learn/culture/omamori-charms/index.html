Omamori: the charms people carry

お守りの選び方

3 min readLast updated: 2026-10-12Cultural review: pending (sample content)

Overview

  • Omamori are small charms for a specific wish, such as safe travel, health, love or passing exams.
  • People hang them on bags or keep them in wallets.
  • Many return an old charm to a shrine or temple after about a year.
